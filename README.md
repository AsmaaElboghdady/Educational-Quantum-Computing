# Educational-Quantum-Computing

Welcome to **Educational Quantum Computing**, a collection of learning resources designed to introduce important concepts in quantum computing.

## Quantum Error Correction (QEC)

This project introduces the fundamental principles of **Quantum Error Correction (QEC)** 

Quantum systems are highly sensitive to environmental noise and operational imperfections. Quantum Error Correction provides methods to detect and correct errors while preserving the quantum information needed for computation.

## Learning Objectives

By studying the presentation, learners will develop an understanding of:

- The motivation for Quantum Error Correction.
- Common sources and types of quantum errors.
- The basic principles of quantum error-correcting codes.
- The role of stabilizers and syndrome measurements.
- An introduction to the **surface code**, an important approach to fault-tolerant quantum computing.

## Presentation

The repository includes a PowerPoint presentation covering Quantum Error Correction through the introductory concepts of the surface code.

**Resource:** QEC PowerPoint presentation (`.pptx`)

## Intended Audience

This material is intended for students, educators, and learners interested in quantum computing, quantum information, and quantum error correction.

Basic familiarity with qubits and introductory quantum computing concepts may be helpful.

## Project Goals

The goal of this repository is to make educational quantum computing resources accessible, organized, and easy to share.

More learning materials may be added as the project develops.
